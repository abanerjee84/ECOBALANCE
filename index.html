The world

A square land divided into cells. Green cones are plants (edible, regrow on their own — a shared commons); brown rocks are ore (finite, can be mined and sold). Each glowing capsule is an agent: it must eat or its energy drains to zero and it dies.

The agents

Every step, each agent picks one of five moves — gather food, trade ore for food, share food with a neighbour, store food safely, or migrate — plus a direction to walk. The colour floating above an agent shows what it just did.

Click any agent to see its inventory, health, and which actions its policy is leaning toward right now.

Nobody is programmed

There is no script. All agents share one brain (a PPO neural network) that is trained online from the scores the environment gives them. Early on they starve a lot; as learning progresses, starvation should collapse toward zero — that falling red curve on the Mortality chart is the proof it works.

Why the brain improves

Each step every agent receives a reward built from: gaining energy (survival), growing food + ore holdings (inventory), growing its safe bank (prosperity), a small alive bonus, and a death penalty — see Reward mapping on the left. Every 32 steps the last batch of experience (~1,900 agent-steps at population 60) is used for a few gradient updates, then thrown away — PPO is on-policy, so it always learns from fresh data rather than a long replay buffer.

Reading the charts (top)

  • Mortality — starvations (red bars) vs. reward (green): the learning story.
  • Resource stocks — how much of the commons is left; a long decline means overharvesting.
  • Ore price / volume — market price of ore in food units, and deals per step.
  • Inequality — Gini coefficient (0 = everyone equal, 1 = one rich agent).
  • Learning — policy entropy (exploration) and critic loss.

Emergence to look for

  • Sharing & reciprocity in Emergence metrics — cooperation with no reward for it.
  • Trade spikes during scarcity — prices jump when food gets tight.
  • Overharvest > 1 — the tragedy of the commons; drop flora regrowth in Config to force it.
  • Rising Gini — wealth concentrating into a few agents.
